# Credits and provenance

## Source

Earthmarks was extracted as a standalone project with fresh Git history from
Ritornello's internal globe-template work. Collection snapshots, scheduling,
review logs, personal annotations, and internal project history are excluded.
The 704-city seed contains only place names, coordinates, types and geographic
contexts. Its scope is curated rather than an exhaustive world-city catalogue.
These factual entries carry no claim of exclusive rights.

## MapLibre GL JS

MapLibre GL JS 6.16.0 is bundled into generated templates together with its
worker and CSS. Its BSD 3-Clause notice is reproduced in
[`licenses/MAPLIBRE.txt`](licenses/MAPLIBRE.txt). MapLibre's bundled dependency
notices remain in the installed package and generated bundle. Keep runtime
attribution visible.

## NASA Blue Marble

The shared satellite overview is derived from the January 2004
[Blue Marble Next Generation base map](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-map/).
NASA credit remains visible in the card. The exact download and digest are
recorded in `places/data/offline-source.json`. These NASA-produced images are
available under [NASA’s image-use policy](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/); NASA names and insignia do not imply
endorsement. The source JPEG and generated tiles are build artifacts, not
committed source files.

## Natural Earth

Optional country reference lines are derived from Natural Earth's 1:110m
admin-0 countries data. Natural Earth data is
[public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).
The coarse borders are a reference aid, not an assertion about disputed territory.

## Media

README screenshots and GIFs show synthetic San Francisco and Paris notes rendered in
actual disposable Anki by anki-addon-workbench. They are cropped to the card and
resized for documentation. No personal notes, review histories or collection
identifiers appear. The banner is original decorative artwork, not a card render.

Photographs and Wikipedia articles added by users keep their original terms.
