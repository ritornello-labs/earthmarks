# Earthmarks 0.1.0 — first shared edition

704 cities on one offline satellite globe. Add a place with just its name and
coordinates. The package contains 704 new cards and all 341 shared imagery tiles.

Import `Earthmarks.apkg` into Anki and let media finish syncing. AnkiDroid users:
enable **Settings → Advanced → localStorage in study screen** to retain settings.

The final native export passed disposable Anki interaction checks: pin removal,
implicit crosshair guess, borders, tolerance and two-pin answer comparison.
Every exported tile was verified against the source; the final artifact passed
the immutable publication checker. The fixed-schema receipt is recorded alongside
this release. Source is MIT licensed; bundled MapLibre, NASA imagery and Natural
Earth borders retain their respective terms. See THIRD_PARTY.md.

AnkiWeb submission is pending secure publisher sign-in. This direct download is
the complete first edition and can be studied immediately.
